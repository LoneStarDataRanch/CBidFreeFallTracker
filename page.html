CigarBid FreeFall Tracker

A free bookmarklet for CigarBid FreeFall auctions. It follows the price loop by loop and shows where this loop will bottom out, right on the auction page. Nothing to install. Version {{VERSION}}.

🍬 FF Tracker

Drag the button to your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B or Cmd+Shift+B shows the bar). Then open a CigarBid FreeFall auction and click the bookmark.

Can’t drag (phone or tablet)? Copy the code, make a new bookmark, and paste it in as the bookmark’s address.

How FreeFall prices move

A FreeFall price doesn’t fall once. It runs in loops: the price drops in even steps (about every 3 seconds) for roughly two minutes, holds at its floor for a few seconds, then resets and starts again. Each loop’s floor is a little different, and now and then one goes noticeably lower. The tracker follows every loop.

What it shows

  • This loop’s low, the last five loop lows, and the usual floor (after two complete loops)
  • A countdown to the bottom of the current loop, or “holding at” while it sits at its floor
  • A target price per tab, with sound and browser alerts, and a warning when your target is below every loop low seen so far
  • Optional auto-buy, always off until you arm it; CigarBid’s “I Confirm this Purchase” box stays your final check
  • Price drop rate, a sparkline, CSV save and load, and it remembers the auction if you refresh
  • Several tabs at once, showing which auction is closest to its target

Use it

  1. Open a CigarBid FreeFall auction and click the bookmark. The panel appears at the top right.
  2. Watch a couple of loops (about 5 minutes) so it learns the usual floor.
  3. Type your target price and press Set.
  4. Only if you want it to buy: tick “I Confirm this Purchase” on the page, then press Arm Auto-Buy.

The floor and countdown are estimates from the loops seen so far, not a promise of where the price will go. If the page freezes when you open DevTools, turn off “pause on debugger statements” (Ctrl+F8 in the Sources tab) and refresh.
